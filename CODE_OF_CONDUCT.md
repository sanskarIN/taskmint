# Code of Conduct

TaskMint welcomes respectful, constructive participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and professional when discussing ideas or reviewing code.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, accessibility needs, and communication styles.
- Protect private information and avoid posting personal task data in issues or screenshots.
- Help keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized comments, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, and repeated disruptive conduct are not welcome.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
